# ElevenLabs pronunciation audio

Coverage: **1,489 of 1,500 headwords**. The unchanged first sprite covers 810 words. The 2026-10-22 sprite adds 679: 660 isolated words and 19 complete example sentences. Sentence clips are marked **例文音声** with the complete spoken sentence in the vocabulary rows and playback status. Eleven words remain on device TTS because recognition was absent or disagreed with the expected word; this does not establish that the original recordings were incorrect. See `recording-manifest.json`, `regenerate-words.txt` and `unmatched-words.json` for the remaining script.

`jessica-map.json` version 2 uses `sources` and `[start,end,sourceIndex]` for the second sprite; the original two-number ranges remain unchanged. `clipMetadata` identifies sentence clips. The old audio bytes and all 810 ranges are preserved. Each new excerpt has 0.08 seconds of added leading silence and 0.42 seconds of trailing silence. Playback ends after the first 0.15 seconds of trailing silence, leaving 0.27 seconds before the following excerpt. Source ranges also retain surrounding silence where available.

The browser streams at most two HTMLAudio elements, one per source, rather than decoding both entire recordings into memory. Playback speed, repeated words, sound loops, next-sound progression and scroll-follow settings apply to both. Failed loading/playback and uncovered words use device TTS. British English uses device TTS. Leaving the page or switching the screen off stops playback; background playback is unsupported.

The new source ranges were checked against the ordered 69 utterances in each of ten recordings, saved ASR, surrounding recognized anchors and waveform silence. Zero-duration ASR timestamps were replaced with physical utterance boundaries. Homophones were assessed as pronunciation matches. These checks are not a human listening audit of all 679 clips. Ambiguous homographs are retained in their full context sentence where safe word isolation was not established.

Reproduction: retain the original uploads at the paths referenced in `new-20261022/part-*/final-candidates.json`; run `python audio/build-extra-session.py`, then `python build-expansion.py`. The latter rebuilds the 1,500-word app, coverage counts and remaining recording batches from the map. `new-20261022/archive/recording-manifest.json` preserves the original 690-entry order; `archive/jessica-map.json` preserves the old 810 ranges. Old 69 pending batches are replaced with two batches for the remaining 11 words. The archive manifest contains all original batch text for comparison.

Validation: JavaScript syntax; independent two-source mock tests (source switching, playback speed, repeat seek/reuse, map/metadata-load cancellation, rejection/unknown/British fallback, hidden-page stop); existing repeat/loop/next-sound tests; map/source bounds and original-range preservation. Real Android playback and a complete individual listening audit remain unverified.
